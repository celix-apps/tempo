1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Tempo app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch handles your data. Tempo is made by Resul Çelik, an individual developer operating as Celix Apps ("we", "us"). It also covers emails you send to our support address.

Apple's services that Tempo works with, such as iCloud, Apple Health and the App Store, are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. What Tempo keeps, and where

Everything below is entered by you or created from your own use of the app. None of it is sent to us.

Streaks, scores, reports and insights are calculated on your device.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Tempo, the data marked above syncs between your devices through Apple's CloudKit. It is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account. We cannot see, read or recover it, and it is never sent to us.

This is your choice. Tempo works fully without iCloud:

  • To turn it off, open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn off Tempo in the list of apps using iCloud.
  • Before you turn iCloud off or sign out of it, we recommend saving a backup in Tempo: Settings › Data and privacy › Export as JSON. iOS may remove synced data from the device when the account changes.
  • Turning sync off does not delete the copy already in your iCloud. To delete it, use Delete all data in Tempo while iCloud is on, or remove Tempo's data under iCloud storage in the Settings app.

Data that you type yourself, such as notes, amounts and injury periods, syncs as you wrote it. If you don't want something in iCloud, don't enter it in those fields, or turn iCloud off for Tempo.

Separately from sync, your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer) may include Tempo's habit data if you have backups on. Tempo's weight log, body measurements and progress photos are left out of device backups.

4. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and needs your permission. You choose each type of data in Apple's permission screen. Everything in Tempo also works by hand, without Health.

Health dataAccessWhy
Workouts, sleep, steps, water, protein, calories consumedReadTo check off the habits you link to Health and show today's progress
Body weightReadTo show your weight log and trend
Body weightWriteTo save the weight you enter in Tempo to Health, if you allow it
  • Health readings are processed on your device. Tempo does not keep a copy of your Health records.
  • When a reading reaches your target, Tempo saves the result: that the habit was completed, with your target amount, and that it came from Health. This check-in is kept like any other check-in, so it syncs with your own iCloud account if you use iCloud with Tempo.
  • If you add progress by hand to a habit linked to Health, Tempo starts from the amount shown on the card, which can include what was read from Health that day, and saves the total you confirm as your own entry. For example, 6,200 steps shown plus 1,000 added by you is saved as 7,200. If you use iCloud with Tempo, this entry is also kept in your own iCloud account.
  • If you set up a weight goal, Tempo may suggest a starting value from your recent weights. The value you save stays on your device.
  • Weight that Tempo writes to Health is then held by Apple Health. If you have turned on Health in iCloud, Apple syncs it under your settings.
  • With your permission, Tempo may also check linked Health data in the background so habits are checked off on time.
  • We never receive your Health data. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

To change or remove access, open the Health app, go to Sharing › Apps › Tempo.

Tempo is not a medical device. Nothing in it is medical advice.

5. Weight, measurements and photos

Your weight log, body measurements, weight and measurement goals and progress photos are stored only on your device. Tempo does not sync them with iCloud, and leaves them out of device backups.

  • Photos are encrypted with a key that is stored only in your device's keychain. They are shown only after Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Location and other details stored inside the picture are removed, and photos taken in Tempo are not added to your photo library.
  • To move this data to another device, you can create a file protected with your own password (Settings › Data and privacy › Export body data). We cannot open this file or recover the password. If you save it to iCloud Drive or another service, that is your choice and that service's terms apply.
  • If the device is lost or erased and you have no such file, this data cannot be recovered.

6. Permissions

Tempo asks only when a feature needs it. You can refuse and keep using the app, and change your mind in the Settings app.

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not marketing messages and contain no health values.
  • Apple Health: see section 4.
  • Camera: only to take progress photos.
  • Photos: you pick a photo in Apple's photo picker. Tempo receives only the photo you pick and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: to unlock the app, if you turn on app lock, and your progress photos. Apple does the check. Tempo only learns whether it succeeded and never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Tempo does not use your location, contacts, microphone or calendar.

7. Widgets, Apple Watch and Shortcuts

  • Widgets and Live Activities read your habits from a storage area on your device that is shared only between Tempo and its own extensions. They can appear on your Lock Screen.
  • The Apple Watch app receives a summary of your habits directly from your paired iPhone through Apple's system.
  • If you use Tempo with Siri or Shortcuts, your habit names are available to iOS so the action can find them. Apple processes the request under its own policy.
  • Share cards are created on your device and go only where you send them.

8. Purchases

Tempo Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, card, billing address or Apple Account details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple gives us sales and subscription reports that do not identify you.

9. Crash reports

Tempo contains no third-party crash or analytics tools. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us crash and performance reports that do not identify you. Technical messages written to the device's system log contain no health values and are not sent to us.

10. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not receive it. The app sends no data to any server of ours. The only services it works with are Apple's, as described above.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. Our mailbox is provided by Apple (iCloud Mail). Please do not send health data or photos you want to keep private. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed. We would disclose an email only if the law required it.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or external fonts of ours; GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address under its privacy statement.

11. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS data protection when you use a passcode. Data in your iCloud account is encrypted by Apple in transit and on its servers. Progress photos and the password-protected body data file are encrypted by Tempo. You can also lock the app. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep a passcode on your device.

CSV and JSON exports are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

12. Exporting, deleting and keeping data

Your data stays until you delete it. In Tempo, open Settings › Data and privacy:

  • Export as JSON: a full backup of your habits, goals and settings, which Restore from backup can open. Export as CSV is available with Pro. Export body data covers weight, measurements and photos.
  • Delete selected data: deletes only the types and dates you choose.
  • Delete all data: deletes everything in Tempo on this device and, when iCloud is on, in your iCloud account. This can't be undone. If iCloud is off, only the device is cleared and the copy in iCloud stays until you delete again with iCloud on.

Delete all data does not remove weight entries saved in Apple Health, files you exported, device backups, or your purchases. You can remove weight entries in the Health app. Random identifiers of deleted habits stay on the device so that sync does not bring them back; they contain no personal content.

Deleting the app removes its data from the device but not from iCloud. If you want both removed, use Delete all data first. Keychain items, such as the photo key, may remain on the device after the app is deleted; they open nothing by themselves. Cancel a subscription in the Settings app: deleting Tempo does not cancel it.

13. Children

Tempo is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher age your country sets for consent. We do not knowingly receive personal data from children. If you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message.

14. Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data yourself in the app, and withdraw any permission in the Settings app. Because we hold no app data, we have none to give you or delete. The only personal data we may hold is an email you sent us. You can ask us to show, correct or delete it, limit its use, or object to its use. We answer within 30 days.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR and similar laws): Resul Çelik (Celix Apps) is the controller for support emails. We process them on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, or in the UK to the ICO.
  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698): you have the rights listed in Article 11 and may complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr).
  • California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. You have the right to know, correct and delete, and we will not treat you differently for using it.
  • Brazil, Canada, Australia and elsewhere: you have the rights your local law gives you, and may contact your local privacy authority.

To use any of these rights, email us. We may ask you to write from the same address to confirm it is yours.
